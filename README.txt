Campus Events Guide — SW302 Assignment 1

Files:
- index.html: home page with three featured events
- events.html: event catalogue with upcoming and past events
- event-details.html: featured event details, local audio, and review form
- schedule.html: timetable with headings
- registration.html: registration form with the requested HTML5 controls
- style.css: shared external stylesheet
- assets/: three original SVG illustrations and a short WAV audio sample

How to open:
1. Extract this folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Use the navigation to visit the five pages.

Important:
- Forms demonstrate browser validation only; they do not send or save submissions.
- The dates, venues, social links, and contact email are sample content. Confirm or change them if your instructor expects specific details.
- The dark mode uses CSS :has() and a checkbox, without JavaScript.
- Read and understand every section before submitting, and make your own changes so you can explain your work during evaluation.
- Add your actual GitHub repository URL to your submission when you create the repository.
